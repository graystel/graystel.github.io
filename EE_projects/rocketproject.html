Currently (June 2026-Present), I am an Avionics RE (responsible engineer) for the analog avionics boards in the Ares team in Rocket Project @ UCLA. I am designing two boards for the avionics system. The first board is for receiving differential pair analog load cell (LC) data from our recovery system, converting the analog values to digital values, and then processing the digital values with an ESP32 P4 Module. The second board achieves the same purpose for faciliating single-ended pressure transducer (PT) data from our engine/tanks.

Previously (Jan 2026-June 2026), I was one of three Avionics Leads for the entire avionics system on the Prometheus team in Rocket Project @ UCLA. This meant that I designed the schematic/PCB for the primary bodytube board (a more primitive version of the current Ares system), and I designed the schematic for the secondary bodytube board which aimed to replicate the nosecone sensors (GPS, accelerometer, LoRA radio module) and conduct telemetry.
