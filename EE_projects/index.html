As a Mathematics of Computation major, my experience with electrical engineering comes almost exclusively from project experience. From Jan 2026 to June 2026, I was one of the Avionics Leads on the Prometheus team (hybrid rocket) in UCLA's flagship rocketry club, Rocket Project @ UCLA. Since June 2026, I have moved up to Avionics RE (responsible engineer) for the Ares team (liquid rocket). In both positions, I have gained hands-on experience with using base electronic components (resistors, capacitors, diodes, etc) and ICs (ESP32s, ADCs, etc). This includes component selection, applying EE first principles alongside datasheet info to design schematics and optimize PCB design, soldering and manufacturing (and debugging) (a lot of debugging) hardware onto these boards. All EE processes were undertaken under the constraints of the overall rocket goal; these constraints range from size, power, wire harnessing, etc. to optimal component selection to balance assurance that our system works and aiming for the highest quality for our avionics system.

Additional EE experience comes from my experience in projects hosted by IEEE @ UCLA. During 2025-2026, I breadboarded circuits with throughhole components to establish the foundational understanding of low level electronic components (writing to an ESP32, using transistors, etc.) I have recently joined IEEE @ UCLA's WRAP (Wireless, RF, and Analog) project, which focuses on faciliating the creation of an RF system without depending on commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) ICs to black-box all of the granular details. I am also currently auditing the DAV (Digital Design, Architecture, and Verification) project for developing a digital circuit!
